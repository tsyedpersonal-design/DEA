Our Mission

Building a Community of
DFW Founders

The Dallas Entrepreneurial Alliance was founded on a simple belief: that great ideas have no age limit, and that the entrepreneurial spirit is alive across every corner of the Dallas–Fort Worth community.

We bring world-class business leaders into the same room as DFW's most ambitious people, to create a direct line between those with deep experience and those with bold aspiration.

Come ready to listen, ask hard questions, and leave with a clearer vision of what's possible.

Featured Speakers

Voices Who've

Sports & Brand Marketing

Jerry Jones Jr.

Executive Vice President
Dallas Cowboys · NFL

One of the most influential executives in professional sports, Jerry Jones Jr. serves as Chief Sales & Marketing Officer of the Dallas Cowboys — the most valuable sports franchise on the planet, valued at over $9 billion.

Jerry Jr. has spent his career at the intersection of sports, entertainment, and business, helping transform the Cowboys from a football team into a global media and lifestyle brand. He has overseen landmark sponsorship deals, the development of AT&T Stadium — one of the world's most sophisticated sports venues — and the Cowboys' evolution into a year-round entertainment property. A graduate of Georgetown University, his story is one of taking an already legendary brand and making it bigger, bolder, and more relevant in an era of rapid change — a challenge every entrepreneur can learn from.

Tech & Consumer Products

Mandy Ginsberg

Former CEO, Match Group
Tinder · Hinge

Mandy Ginsberg is one of the most decorated executives in the history of consumer technology. As CEO of Match Group — parent of Tinder, OKCupid, Hinge and dozens more — she oversaw a portfolio reaching hundreds of millions of users in over 190 countries.

Mandy spent nearly two decades at Match Group, rising from VP of Marketing to President of Match North America to CEO of the entire portfolio. She is widely credited with modernizing Match.com and building a data-driven product culture. Under her leadership, Tinder became one of the most downloaded apps in the world. Named one of Fortune's Most Powerful Women in Business, Mandy is a passionate advocate for women in leadership and authentic company culture. She now serves on several boards and actively mentors the next generation of business leaders.

Clean Energy & Innovation

Kevin Fine

Co-Founder, Lunar Batteries

Kevin Fine is a clean energy entrepreneur who played a pivotal role in one of the most consequential product launches in modern technology — the Tesla Powerwall. As head of Tesla's Powerwall division, he helped bring home battery storage from concept to millions of households.

At Tesla, Kevin was part of the energy division during a transformational period — racing to prove that sustainable energy at home wasn't just possible, but desirable and accessible. After Tesla, he co-founded Lunar Energy, a next-generation home energy company building integrated solar, battery, and smart home systems for complete energy independence. Lunar Energy has raised significant venture funding and is widely regarded as one of the most exciting companies in clean energy. Kevin's career shows how entrepreneurial thinking is reshaping one of the world's most important industries.
